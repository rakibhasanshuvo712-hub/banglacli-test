Our story

Grown with passion,
delivered with love

What began as a tiny stall at the corner of a Dhaka market has grown into a studio built around one simple belief: flowers should mean something. Every stem we choose and every ribbon we tie is a little love letter to the person on the other end.

✿

Picked at their peak

We visit our partner farms each morning and choose only the freshest stems.

✦

Arranged by hand

Every bouquet is tied by a florist who cares about the little details.

❋

Chilled, wrapped and hand-delivered so it arrives as beautiful as it left.
